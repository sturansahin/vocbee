Privacy policy for VocBee.

This page summarizes how VocBee handles account data, learning progress, local storage, reminder preferences, and third-party services used to support the mobile app.

What we collect

Account details, learning progress, favorites, language choices, reminder settings, and limited device data needed for ads and operations.

Why we use it

To authenticate users, store progress, personalize vocabulary delivery, send reminders, and improve app reliability.

Your controls

You can update profile data, change reminder settings, reset progress, or request deletion of your account and associated data.

1. Information we collect

VocBee may collect account registration data, optional Google Sign-In profile data, profile information, learning progress, favorites, selected languages, and notification preferences.

  • Email address, display name, and authentication data handled through Firebase Authentication.
  • Progress data such as current level, learned words, favorites, and last viewed word position.
  • Device-related information used by third-party services, including operating system version and advertising identifiers where permitted.

2. How we use data

We use collected information to create and manage accounts, save learning progress, synchronize preferences, deliver reminders, support AI-powered grammar explanations, and improve the app.

3. Storage and third parties

VocBee stores some information locally on device and uses external services for authentication, sync, ads, AI explanations, and notifications.

You can update profile information and reminder settings from the app. You may also request deletion of your account and associated data.
