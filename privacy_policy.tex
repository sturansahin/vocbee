\section{Introduction}

Welcome to VocBee ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application VocBee (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

\section{Information We Collect}

\subsection{Personal Information You Provide}

When you register for an account or use our services, we may collect the following information:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item \textbf{Account Registration Data:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Email address
        \item Full name (display name)
        \item Password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication)
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Google Sign-In Data} (if you choose to sign in with Google):
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Google account email address
        \item Google profile name
        \item Google profile photo URL
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Profile Information:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Profile picture (optional, user-uploaded)
        \item Display name
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Learning and Usage Data}

We automatically collect certain information when you use the App:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item \textbf{Learning Progress:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Current proficiency level (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2)
        \item Words marked as "learned"
        \item Words marked as "skipped"
        \item Favorite words list
        \item Last word position for session continuity
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Language Preferences:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Native language selection
        \item Target language selection
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Notification Preferences:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Notification enabled/disabled status
        \item Notification time range (start and end hours)
        \item Notification frequency settings
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{App Usage Data:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item App launch count
        \item Last sync timestamp
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Device Information}

We may collect device-related information through our third-party advertising partner:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item Device type and model
    \item Operating system version
    \item Advertising identifier (for personalized ads, if permitted)
    \item IP address (anonymized for analytics)
\end{itemize}

\section{How We Use Your Information}

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item \textbf{Account Management:} To create and manage your user account, authenticate your identity, and enable login across devices.
    
    \item \textbf{Learning Experience:} To track your vocabulary learning progress, provide personalized word recommendations, and maintain continuity across sessions.
    
    \item \textbf{Data Synchronization:} To sync your learning progress and preferences across multiple devices via cloud storage.
    
    \item \textbf{Notifications:} To send vocabulary reminder notifications at your preferred times to help you learn consistently.
    
    \item \textbf{AI-Powered Features:} To provide grammar explanations and example sentence analysis using artificial intelligence.
    
    \item \textbf{Advertising:} To display advertisements that help keep the App free to use.
    
    \item \textbf{App Improvement:} To analyze usage patterns and improve the App's functionality and user experience.
\end{itemize}

\section{Data Storage and Security}

\subsection{Local Storage}

We store certain data locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item Learning progress and preferences
    \item Vocabulary cache for offline access
    \item Authentication tokens
    \item Notification settings
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Cloud Storage}

We use Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) to securely store and synchronize your data:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item \textbf{Firebase Authentication:} Manages user accounts and authentication credentials with industry-standard security practices.
    
    \item \textbf{Firebase Firestore:} Stores user profiles, learning progress, and favorites with encryption at rest and in transit.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Security Measures}

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item Data encryption in transit (TLS/SSL)
    \item Data encryption at rest
    \item Secure authentication protocols
    \item Regular security assessments
\end{itemize}

\section{Data Retention}

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item \textbf{Account Data:} Retained until you delete your account
    \item \textbf{Learning Progress:} Retained until you delete your account or clear your data
    \item \textbf{Local Cache:} Cleared when you log out or uninstall the App
\end{itemize}
