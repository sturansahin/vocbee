# Privacy Policy

**VocBee – Vocabulary Learning Application**  
_Last Updated: March 2026_

---

## Introduction

Welcome to **VocBee** ("we," "our," or "us").  
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the VocBee mobile application (the **App**).

For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, including **GDPR** and **KVKK**, the data controller is:

**Sahi Labs**

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information You Provide

When you register or use our services, we may collect:

- **Account Registration Data:** Email address, full name, and password (securely handled via Firebase Authentication).
- **Third-Party Sign-In Data (Google & Apple):** Email, profile name, and profile photo URL if you choose to sign in using Google or Apple Sign-In.
- **Profile Information:** Optional display name and profile picture. To upload a profile picture, the App may request access to your device's **Camera** and **Photo Library**.

---

### Learning and Usage Data

We automatically collect:

- **Learning Progress:** Proficiency level (A1–C2), learned or skipped words, favorite words, and session continuity.
- **Preferences:** Native and target language selections, notification time ranges and frequencies.
- **App Usage Data:** App launch count, last synchronization timestamp, and anonymized usage analytics.

---

### Device Information and Permissions

We may collect device-related information and request specific device permissions to provide our services and display advertisements:

- **Device Information:** Device type, model, operating system version, and anonymized IP address for analytics and security.
- **Microphone / Audio (Android):** The App may request audio recording permissions (`RECORD_AUDIO`) for features related to pronunciation or speech interaction. We do not store or transmit your audio data to our servers.
- **Advertising Identifier and Tracking (IDFA / AAID):** We collect your device's advertising identifier to display personalized ads.
  - **iOS Users:** Under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, we explicitly ask for your permission ("Allow VocBee to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites") before using your IDFA for personalized advertising.
  - **GDPR / EU Users:** We utilize the User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain explicit consent for data processing related to personalized ads.

---

## Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data under the following legal bases:

- **Consent:** For notifications, device tracking (ATT), and personalized advertising preferences (UMP).
- **Contractual Necessity:** To provide vocabulary learning services and synchronize data.
- **Legitimate Interests:** To improve app performance, analyze usage, and maintain security.
- **Legal Obligation:** To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Manage user accounts and authenticate identity.
- Track learning progress and provide personalized learning recommendations.
- Synchronize learning data across your devices.
- Send vocabulary reminders based on user preferences.
- Provide AI-powered grammar explanations and sentence analysis.
- Display both personalized and non-personalized advertisements to support free access to the App.

## Data Storage and Security

### Storage

- **Local Storage:** Vocabulary cache, user settings, and learning progress are saved locally on your device (via AsyncStorage) for offline access and faster loading.
- **Cloud Storage (Firebase):** Secure and encrypted storage of user data to enable cross-device synchronization.

### Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including TLS/SSL encryption and secure authentication mechanisms.  
However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

---

## International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed on servers located outside your country, including the **United States** and **European Union**.

We ensure appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses and secure transfer mechanisms in compliance with GDPR and KVKK.
