Delete Your Account

At VocBee, we value your privacy and give you full control over your data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you permanently remove your:

  • Profile information (name, email, photo)
  • Learning progress and statistics
  • Saved vocabulary and favorites
  • Settings and preferences
Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your progress will be lost.

Option 1: Delete from App (Recommended)

If you still have the app installed, this is the fastest way:

  1. Open VocBee app.
  2. Go to Settings > Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the action.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot access it, you can request account deletion by contacting our support team. We will process your request within 7 days.
